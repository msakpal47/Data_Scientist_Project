Timestamp			Time of measurement (mm:ss.ms format)
Locality			Name of the area or neighborhood in Bihar
Latitude			GPS latitude of the test location
Longitude			GPS longitude of the test location
Signal Strength (dBm)		Measured signal strength in decibel-milliwatts (target column for regression)
Signal Quality (%)		Quality of signal as percentage (0–100%)
Data Throughput (Mbps)		Download speed at the time of measurement
Latency (ms)			Network response delay in milliseconds
Network Type			Type of network used (e.g., 3G, 4G, LTE, 5G)
BB60C Measurement (dBm)		Signal strength captured by the BB60C SDR device
srsRAN Measurement (dBm)	Signal strength measured via srsRAN software stack
BladeRFxA9 Measurement (dBm)	Signal measurement from BladeRFxA9 hardware
