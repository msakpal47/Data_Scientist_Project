Store - The unique identifier for each store.

DayOfWeek - Represents the day of the week as a numerical value.
(0 = Monday, 6 = Sunday).

Date - The calendar date for the sales record in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

Sales - The total revenue (in monetary units) generated by the store on a specific day.

Customers - The total number of customers who visited the store on a specific day.

Open - A binary indicator showing whether the store was open (1) or closed (0) on a specific day.

Promo - A binary indicator showing whether the store was running a promotion (1) or not (0) on a specific day.

StateHoliday - Denotes whether the day was a public holiday (e.g., 'a', 'b', 'c', or '0' for no holiday).

SchoolHoliday - A binary indicator showing whether the day was a school holiday (1) or not (0).
